Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Draw Diagrams (draw.ibrahimhz.com) is a personal diagram editor built on Excalidraw. This policy explains what data the app collects, why it collects it, and how it is handled.

Information we collect

  • Google account information: Google account ID, email address, display name, and profile picture when you sign in with Google.
  • Diagram metadata: diagram names, descriptions, timestamps, ownership, and Google Drive file identifiers.
  • Diagram content: the Excalidraw scene you save, including elements, app state, and embedded files. Diagram content is stored as .excalidraw files in your own Google Drive.
  • Session data: a hashed session token and expiry time. The session cookie itself is HttpOnly and is not readable by JavaScript.
  • Google authorization data: an OAuth refresh token, encrypted before storage, used to access Google Drive on your behalf.

How Google user data is used

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This allows the app to create and access only the files and folders it creates in your Google Drive. It does not grant access to your entire Drive.

  • Sign you in and identify your account.
  • Create an Excalidraw folder in your Drive.
  • Create, read, update, and delete the diagram files you save.
  • Show your diagram list and load selected diagrams.

Draw Diagrams' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

  • Google Drive: diagram files and the Excalidraw folder.
  • Neon PostgreSQL: account details, diagram metadata, hashed sessions, and the encrypted refresh token.
  • Hosting providers: Netlify serves the frontend, Vercel runs the API, and Google provides authentication and Drive.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to operate the app, or when required by law.

Cookies

  • session: keeps you signed in. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, expires after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • oauth_state: protects the Google sign-in flow. Expires after 10 minutes.

No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

Security

The app uses HTTPS, HttpOnly cookies, encrypted refresh tokens, server-side ownership checks, and hashed session tokens. No method is perfectly secure, but reasonable measures are taken to protect your data.

Data retention and deletion

  • Deleting a diagram removes its metadata and its Google Drive file.
  • Signing out deletes the current session.
  • You can revoke the app's Google access at Google Account permissions.
  • To delete your account data, email ibrahim.nerdcentra@gmail.com. Account records and metadata will be removed. Diagram files remain in your Google Drive unless you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes
